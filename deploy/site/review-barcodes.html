元辉账本 · 审核用条码

给 App 审核用：用审核号登录「演示小店」后，在「卖出」或「进货」页把手机摄像头对准电脑屏幕上的条码即可扫入。扫不出时，点取景框下面的「手输条码」，输入条码下面的数字。

For App Review: sign in with the review account (it opens the demo store), go to “卖出” (Sell) or “进货” (Stock in) and point the phone camera at a barcode on this screen. If scanning is inconvenient, tap “手输条码” (Enter barcode) under the viewfinder and type the digits shown under the barcode.
